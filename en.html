DECLARED GAPS
  • overfit_RL· of the k=5 real decoders/priors for this experiment, si1000 (6.94%, headline) is the WORST, not the best — the real minimum is libra+RL (6.11%). The split above (0–40k/40k–50k) doesn't show RL worse on test than on train, but that's weak evidence: if Google calibrated the RL prior on these same shots, the split is contaminated and proves nothing beyond this ZIP (R180/R192).
  • source gap· the DataCite record for 13273331 (the dataset) does NOT cite back to 08449-y (the paper) — it only cites two 2021/2022 papers. The citation is one-directional: the paper cites the dataset (CR60), the dataset doesn't cite the paper. Verified with two real GET requests (DataCite + Crossref), not assumed from the spec (R146/R148).
  • cone· the five decoders/priors in this dataset ran in batch over already-pooled shots, not in real time against the chip (R125). Splitting the .b8 into sequential chunks (above) shows the batch isn't hiding a large drift — but that is NOT the same as decoding online against the chip: it's still the same closed file, split after the fact.
  • different ê, same Y· at r=10, k=5 real decoder/prior combinations give different ê on the SAME Y: 6.94% (correlated+si1000, headline) vs 6.39% (correlated+RL) vs 6.79/6.17/6.11% (harmony/libra). This is not "the tape changed" (R127/R129).
  • fitted ε, not the official Λ· the ε≈0.78%/cycle in the chart comes from my own fit 0.5−0.5(1−2ε)ʳ to 15 points — it is not the Λ metric the paper reports (which compares across distances, not just cycles). Max fit residual: 2.6 percentage points (R133).
  • p_th· a single d isn't enough to know whether this chip is below threshold; d=5 and d=7 are needed too (also in this ZIP, not loaded in this strip).
  • physical→logical map· no Lindblad L=√γσ₋ is declared for this chip; the decoders see Pauli syndromes, not physical levels (R124).
